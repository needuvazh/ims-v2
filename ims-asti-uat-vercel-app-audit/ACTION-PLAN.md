# SEO Action Plan: ims-asti-uat.vercel.app

## Phase 1: Critical Fixes (Week 1)

- Confirm indexability intent for `ims-asti-uat.vercel.app`; noindex UAT or migrate to the final branded domain with redirects.
- Fix canonical URLs on `/privacy` and `/terms`; review noindex utility canonicals.
- Remove public crawl paths to auth utility pages and demo-login copy.
- Add missing security headers and remove `X-Powered-By`.

## Phase 2: High-Impact Improvements (Weeks 2-3)

- Rewrite long/generic titles and reused descriptions.
- Fix H1 structure on `/verify` and auth templates.
- Expand thin indexable pages or mark them noindex if they are not organic targets.
- Add richer LocalBusiness and page-specific schema.

## Phase 3: Content & Authority (Month 2)

- Deepen each course page with syllabus, equipment, assessment, duration, eligibility, PPE, and certification details.
- Add proof-oriented E-E-A-T content: accreditation, instructors, safety process, client/corporate training examples.
- Add `/llms.txt` and AI-citable fact blocks.
- Build local citations and authority links on the final production domain.

## Phase 4: Monitoring & Iteration (Ongoing)

- Re-run Lighthouse/PSI when rate limits clear or API credentials are available.
- Connect GSC/GA4 and monitor impressions, indexed pages, local queries, and conversions.
- Capture an SEO drift baseline after fixes.
- Schedule monthly crawl checks for canonicals, noindex, sitemap, title, schema, and broken links.
